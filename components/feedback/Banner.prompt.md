Site-wide announcement strip, the black bar above the header.

```jsx
<Banner tone="ink" icon="truck">Free shipping · Skip or cancel anytime</Banner>
```

- Copy is ALL CAPS and under eight words.
- State a standing term, never a deadline. No countdowns, no "ends tonight", no stock counts.
- Shipping is free with no threshold, so never write a minimum-spend line.
- One banner per page, maximum.
