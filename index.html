The six rules

01
Prices are integers

$49, never $49.00, never $49.99. Savings in dollars, never percentages.

02
20px body, 500 weight

The floor, not the suggestion. 17px is legal and meta only.

03
Black on white

Yellow is the accent. Orange, blue and green code product families, they are not decoration.

04
Accents take black text

White on yellow is forbidden. All four accents carry ink.

05
12px corners, 48px targets

Soft, never pills. Nothing smaller than a comfortable tap.

06
No emoji, no gradients

No bounce, no parallax, no “anti-ageing”.

Still missing

  • Logo files, the wordmark is set in type; nothing has been drawn.
  • Licensed brand fonts. Outfit and Figtree are stand-ins.
  • A brand icon set. Lucide at 2px stroke is the stand-in.
  • Product photography, every colour block is a labelled placeholder.
  • Real product names, prices and review counts.
